## Hi, I'm Kim! 👋

I'm a computer science student and aspiring software engineer currently building my skills through coursework, 
hands-on projects, and independent learning. 

Before beginning my journey into computer science, I spent 11 years working as a dental hygienist. My experience
in healthcare taught me how to solve problems in fast-paced environments, communicate complex information 
clearly, pay close attention to detail, and understand how technology can improve everyday workflows.

My interest in software development grew from years of working with dental practice management systems. While these
systems were technically capable, I noticed a disconnect between how the software functioned and how clinical 
providers actually worked. Simple tasks could require inefficient workflows that didn't always reflect what 
happened chairside. That experience made me curious about what software could look like if the people building it
deeply understood the people using it. It ultimately inspired me to learn how to build software that doesn't just 
work, but *works for the people who use it.*
